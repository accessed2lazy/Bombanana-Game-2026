## 🕹️ About

BOMBANANA! is a comedic three-player online co-op bomb-defusal puzzle game from Lefto Studio, published by TARK. Players take on the roles of blind, deaf, and mute monkeys inside a mobile bomb workshop, combining incomplete information to solve bomb puzzles before time runs out. The Windows release is scheduled for September 2, 2026.

BOMBANANA! is made for friend groups who enjoy chaotic cooperative puzzles, communication challenges, and comedic party games.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 💣 **Bomb Defusal** — Solve bomb modules and defuse explosives against a ticking clock.
- 🐒 **Asymmetric Roles** — Play as a blind, deaf, or mute monkey with unique limitations.
- 🗣️ **Limited Communication** — Share partial information and coordinate under deliberately restricted communication.
- 👥 **Three-Player Co-op** — Work together as a fixed team of three players.
- ⏱️ **Race Against Time** — Make quick decisions before the bomb timer reaches zero.
- 🧩 **Puzzle Solving** — Translate observations and instructions into precise actions.
- 🎉 **Party Game Chaos** — Turn teamwork, confusion, and close calls into comedic game-night moments.
